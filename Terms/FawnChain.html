Fawn-Chain

A Fawn-Chain occours when multiple Fawns threaten each other beacuse they are threatened

   I.E. A black Horse threatens a white Fawn, threatening a black Fawn

Note that if no Fawns in a Fawn-Chain are threatened by a non-Fawn piece, all Fawns in the Fawn-chain are considred to not be threatened

   I.E. If the black Horse from the preivious example moved such that it was no longer threatening the white Fawn, both of the Fawns would be considred not threatened
