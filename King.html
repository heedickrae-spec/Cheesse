Piece info

Piece max: 1

Piece: King

Piece notation: "K"


Movment / Capturing

Chess king


Special

Bridging: If a horse is orthognal to the king and the first two squares from the king to the horse are unocupied, the king may move two spaces towards the horse and move the horse to the opposite side of the king. Note that this is a king move, not a horse move. Note that this move may be done even if the king is mounting a pedestal
