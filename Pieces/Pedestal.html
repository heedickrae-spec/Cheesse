Pedestal

Piece info

Piece max: 1

Piece: Rook

Piece notation: "P"


Movment / Capturing

When not mounted: one space orthognally

When mounted: None


Special

A adjacent ally or enemy piece may mount (be placed on) a pedestal, if said piece can move.

Mounting pieces retain all special effects. I.E. a Cup that is mounting a Pedestal sill makes adjacent pieces Drunk

When a mounted pedestal is captured, the capturing piece is captured and the mounting piece takes place of the pedestal

If a Goliath mounts a pedestal, the pedestal is destroyed and the Goliath takes its place
